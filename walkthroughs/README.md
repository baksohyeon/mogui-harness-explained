# Walkthroughs

Three procedures followed end to end, about fifteen minutes each. The unit pages explain one mechanism at a time; these pages put the mechanisms in the order they run, with the host program, Orca, named at every step it takes. Read the [Orca primer](../concepts/orca-primer.md) first if the words Run, task, dispatch, and pane are new.

| Walkthrough | Steps | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| [One dispatch](01-one-dispatch.md) | 15 | From the owner's word to a closed pane: contract, gate, delivery, four Orca calls, what the wrapper refuses to assume, the worker's side, the master's verification. |
| [One master generation](02-one-master-generation.md) | 11 | From a promotion audit to the owner's word to succeed: placement proof, role state, self-check, retirement by three measured disappearances, compaction. |
| [One template upgrade](03-one-template-upgrade.md) | 12 | From a release tag to a redeployed card: the template-check line, classification, the apply tool's dry run and refusals, hand merges, the test lane, the pull request. |

Every evidence link points at the public repository at tag `v0.5.288`. Measured results come from one seat and are labeled as such; step counts and the counts quoted from source files are not measurements. Identifiers are synthetic.
